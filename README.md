# AI Governance Starter Kit

Five free, plain-language templates for small businesses that want to use AI tools without a compliance team. Built by [Sunnyworks.AI](https://sunnyworks.ai) for founder-led businesses — no email required, nothing sent anywhere, fill them in and use them today.

These are the same resources live as interactive pages at [sunnyworks.ai/resources](https://sunnyworks.ai), reformatted here as plain markdown so they're easy to fork, edit, and drop into your own docs.

## What's in here

| Template | What it's for | Time to fill in |
|---|---|---|
| [`ai-and-tool-inventory.md`](./ai-and-tool-inventory.md) | List every tool your business uses, who owns it, and whether its AI features can see client data. | ~30 min |
| [`safe-input-guide.md`](./safe-input-guide.md) | Three plain-language bands for what's safe to type into an AI tool — and what never is. | ~15 min |
| [`ai-use-policy-starter.md`](./ai-use-policy-starter.md) | A one-page AI use policy your team will actually read. | ~20 min |
| [`decision-log.md`](./decision-log.md) | One written place for decisions made, exceptions allowed, and open questions. | ~10 min to start |
| [`30-day-governance-starter.md`](./30-day-governance-starter.md) | A four-week plan that ties the other four templates together, half an hour at a time. | 4 weeks, ~30 min/week |

**Suggested order:** start with the 30-Day Governance Starter — it sequences the other four for you.

## Why these exist

Most AI governance advice is written for enterprises with a compliance department. These templates summarize, in plain language, ideas from the [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) and [CISA's small-business resources](https://www.cisa.gov/), scaled down to something a five-person team can actually finish in an afternoon.

## Disclaimer

Educational operating guidance only. Not legal advice, cybersecurity certification, or regulatory compliance certification.
