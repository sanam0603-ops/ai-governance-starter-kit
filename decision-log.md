# Decision Log

*Decisions, exceptions, and open questions — in one written place.*

What was decided, when, by whom, why, and when to look again, plus the exceptions you've allowed and the decisions you haven't made yet. The whole memory of how your business chooses, on one page.

**Time:** ~10 minutes to start · Works offline · No email required

## Why one log

Every business runs on decisions, most just never get written down. Six months later they get re-argued from memory, or quietly re-made by a renewal date. One log, five columns, ends that: what was decided, when, by whom, why, and when it's worth another look. The examples below show the size a good entry should be — one honest line, not a memo.

## Using it well

- **Backfill three.** Start by writing down the last three decisions you remember making — the log feels real immediately.
- **One line beats none.** If a decision feels too small to log, log it in ten words. The habit is the asset.
- **Read it quarterly.** The "revisit when" column only works if something revisits it — pair this log with a review date.
