# 30-Day Governance Starter

*Governed in thirty days — half an hour at a time.*

Week one you see the whole stack. Week two you draw the data line. Week three you name owners and make the overdue decisions. Week four you make it a habit. Nothing here takes longer than thirty minutes.

**Time:** 4 weeks · ~30 min per task · Works offline · No email required

## How this works

Four weeks, one theme per week, nothing longer than half an hour at a time. Every task uses one of the other templates in this kit. Check things off as you go. By day 30 you'll have a written inventory, a never-enter list, named owners, a decision log, a one-page policy, and a review date. That's a governed business, at small-business scale.

## Week 1 — See it

- [ ] List every tool and subscription in the **AI & Tool Inventory**. 30 minutes with your statements open. Messy and complete beats neat and partial.
- [ ] Fill the cost column and add it up. The total is the argument for everything else in this plan.
- [ ] Mark the AI columns — features on? what can they read? Every "Unsure" you mark is a real finding. Don't resolve them yet; just mark them.

## Week 2 — Draw the line

- [ ] Adapt the **Safe Input Guide**'s never-enter list to your business. Fifteen minutes. Specific beats generic — name your own folders and files.
- [ ] Share the list with everyone who uses AI tools. Read it aloud once. The questions people ask are the list working.
- [ ] Check the settings of your two most-used AI tools. What do they retain? Do they train on inputs? Personal and business plans often differ.

## Week 3 — Name owners & decide

- [ ] Fill the owner column in the inventory, one name per tool. Blanks are findings. A tool nobody owns is a charge nobody will ever question.
- [ ] Start the **Decision Log** with three backfilled decisions. The last three you remember making. Then log the next real one the day it happens.
- [ ] Make one duplicate-tool decision. Two tools doing one job? Keep one, or write down why you keep both. Either is governance.

## Week 4 — Make it a habit

- [ ] Fill in the **AI Use Policy Starter** — one page, five sections. Twenty minutes, mostly deleting what doesn't apply.
- [ ] Put a recurring half-hour "operating review" in the calendar. Quarterly is enough: re-read the inventory, the list, the log, the policy.
- [ ] Tell the team what exists now, in one message. Four links, one sentence each. Governance nobody knows about is décor.

## Day 30 — what you now have

- A written inventory of every tool, its cost, its owner, and what its AI can read.
- A never-enter list your team has actually seen.
- A decision log with real entries, and a place for exceptions.
- A one-page AI policy with a named owner and a review date.
- The habit that keeps all of it true: one half-hour, once a quarter.
